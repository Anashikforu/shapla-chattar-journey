# Motijheel reality notes (research, Oct 2026)

Gathered from news articles, Wikipedia, seat61 and YouTube auto-caption transcripts. Nobody watched video footage. `[U]` = unverified or general knowledge.

## Getting from Shapla Chattar to Notre Dame College (~1 km)

| Mode | Cost | Realistic flow |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | free | 12-15 min [U]. Footpaths are full of hawkers (Motijheel is the worst of five commercial districts for walking), so people walk in the road. Foot overbridges are partly blocked by stalls and beggars. |
| Rickshaw | asks Tk 40-50, settles at Tk 30-40 | Hail "Mama, jaben?" → "Notre Dame, koto?" → "Ponchash" → "Eto na, tirish dibo" → "Uthen". Pay on arrival: "Ekhane rakhen". Puller may refuse: "Jabo na". About +Tk 20 for well-dressed passengers, -Tk 10 for students. Officially banned on main roads, weakly enforced; side lanes, with a chance a sergeant waves them off. |
| CNG | meter Tk 40 for the first 2 km (2015 rate), but 98% run on "contract"; short trips Tk 100-150 or refused | "Meter-e jaben?" → "Meter-e jabo na, dersho" ("no meter, 150"). Several refusals before one agrees (Prothom Alo, Apr 2026). |
| Local bus | Tk 10 minimum (Sept 2026 gazette, Tk 2.70/km); students half fare | Stops anywhere (one rider counted 40 stops against 8 official). Helper calls: "Ostad, bame plastic", "Ladies nambe", "Jomaiya chalan", "Mama, chap den, jayga ase", "Bhara den", "Pichhone jan". Bangs the side. 9 women's seats behind the driver. Routes: BRTC Pink Bus (women-only, Aug 2026, via Kamalapur-Arambagh-Shapla Chattar), BRTC Motijheel-Taltola, Bikolpo Motijheel-Mirpur 12. |
| Other | | Leguna Motijheel-Mugda; Pathao bikes cluster at junctions. Arambagh is a long-distance bus hub and badly jammed at school times. |

## Kamalapur Railway Station

- **Building:** shell "parasol" roof, open-sided concourse, 8 platforms (Wikipedia), 11 counters, LED departure boards, mosque, railway police. ~115,000 passengers a day.
- **Porters (kuli):** ~250, led by a sardar. Tk 15 a bag under 28 kg, Tk 35 up to 56 kg, trolley Tk 30. Sleep on the floor at night. "Kuli lagbe?" [U]. Red shirt and brass badge unconfirmed [U].
- **People:** tea and snack hawkers ("Cha, gorom cha!"), street children collecting bottles, homeless people sleeping on platforms, RAB/GRP/Ansar. At Eid: three ticket checks (gate, platform, door) and people riding on the roofs (tolerated in 2024-25).
- **Trains:** 13-20 coaches [U], not 8. Sonar Bangla 07:00 Chattogram, Parabat 06:20 Sylhet, Ekota 10:15 Panchagarh, Chattala 14:15 Chattogram, Subarna 16:30 Chattogram, Turna Nishitha ~23:00 Chattogram [U]. Maitree Express to Kolkata is suspended (talks on restarting, Sept 2026).
  - **Coaches:** INKA light green/white/red; Korean stainless steel with colour stripes; older dark green or blue with barred windows [U].
  - **Locomotives:** boxy diesels in yellow and green [U].
- **Tickets:** online with NID (Rail Sheba [U]); standing tickets at the counter on the day (up to 25% of seats). Dhaka-Chattogram fares: Shovan Chair Tk 405-450, Snigdha Tk 855-943, AC seat Tk 1,025.
- **Announcement [U]:** "Jatri shadharoner drishti akorshon korchhi… Chattogramgami antonogor Subarna Express trainti tin nombor platform theke chhere jabe."
- **Forecourt:** rickshaw and CNG drivers overcharge, touts shout "Motijheel! Gulistan! CNG lagbe?", "parking toll" extortion of Tk 2-5.
- **MRT Line 6 extension:** about 77% built; passenger service April 2027. Draw it as a nearly finished elevated station.

## Eating

- **Bhaat hotel:**
  - Food in big pots at the front, visible kitchen, cashier at the cash box by the door. You pay on the way out.
  - The waiter is called "mama" (formally "mesiar") and calls out the total [U].
  - Rice refills are often unlimited ("petchukti"). Dal is free-Tk 10.
  - Lunch rush 1-3pm, full of bank staff, drivers and students; people wait for seats.
  - Many office-area stalls open Sunday-Thursday only ("shukro-shonibar bade"). On Friday the empty Dilkusha streets become pitches.
- **2025-26 prices (Tk):**
  - Egg + rice 60; chicken + rice 80; beef + rice 100
  - Stall curries: murgi 80, beef 180, rui 80, ilish 80, pangash 60, pabda 120
  - Alu bhorta 10; fried egg 20
  - Office "Chinese" plate 100-120; chola biryani set 150
  - Luchi + dal 35; singara 10; paratha 10-15; tea 10-15 [U]
- **Mid-range restaurants:**
  - Hirajheel: khichuri Tk 230-250; kebabs and naan in the evening
  - Ghoroa: bhuna khichuri, since 1979
  - Deshbandhu: fish Tk 130, mutton Tk 170
  - Kacchi Bhai: mutton kacchi Tk 330
  - Star Kabab: paratha Tk 30, tikka Tk 240
- **Fine dining:** Hotel Purbani (Dilkusha); no prices found.

## Football

- **Arambagh "balur math"** (next to BFF House):
  - Artificial turf, relaid Oct 2025. Used from dawn to dusk, with up to 6 groups at once including cricket academies.
  - Locals play from about 3pm into the night; boots required. The national team trains there 2-3 days a week.
  - Floods in the monsoon; one corner is used as a urinal. Eid prayers are held there.
- **Kamalapur Stadium:** turf with floodlights (11 ballasts stolen). About 450 shops crowd the gates, plus rickshaw garages. Called a haven for drug users, with one guard per shift.
- **Paltan / Outer Stadium:** dust, no grass. Rugby, cricket nets and football all at once; mud in the rain.
- **Other games:** "khep" (paid pickup matches, pros included). Colony fields for teenagers. Friday street games in Dilkusha.
- **Shouts [U]:** "Pass de!", "Mar!", "Gol!", "Foul!", "Bol de".
